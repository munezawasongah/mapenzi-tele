Privacy Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Operated by Jatelo Technologies Limited
We keep this simple: we collect only what the Service needs, we never sell your data, and other members never see your exact location.

1. Who we are

mapenziTELE (“the Service”) is operated by Jatelo Technologies Limited (“Jatelo”, “we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal information described here. Contact us any time at support@mapenzitele.co.ke.

2. Information we collect

CategoryWhat
AccountName or nickname, email address, password (stored only as a one-way hash, never in plain text), age, gender, who you are looking for, and what you want right now.
ProfileCity, occupation, tagline, bio, vibes you select, and the photos you upload.
LocationYour device’s GPS coordinates, only if you tap “enable location”. We store them to sort profiles by distance.
ActivitySparks you send and receive, matches, notifications, the messages and photos you send in chats, whether you are online, and when you were last active.

We do not ask for, and you should not post, government ID numbers, financial details or other sensitive identifiers. Information you choose to put in your bio or photos (for example about your sexuality or relationship preferences) is visible to other members.

3. How we use your information

  • To create your account and run the Service: show your profile, match you, deliver chats and notifications.
  • To sort and show profiles by distance.
  • To keep the Service safe: detect abuse, enforce our Terms and Safety Policy, and prevent fraud.
  • To send service notifications (matches, sparks, messages). We do not send marketing without your consent.
  • To comply with the law and respond to valid legal requests.

Our legal bases are: your consent (for example location and notifications, which you can withdraw), performance of our agreement with you, our legitimate interest in running a safe service, and legal obligation.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other members can see your name, age, city, mood, tagline, bio, occupation, vibes, photos, online status and last-active time. They see an approximate distance from you, rounded to the nearest kilometre. They never receive your coordinates.
  • Your matches can read and reply to the messages and photos you send them.
  • Service providers that process data for us under our instructions: cloud hosting and database providers (Railway), and push-notification delivery (Google Firebase Cloud Messaging).
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect someone from serious harm.

We do not sell your personal information and we do not show third-party advertising.

5. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted

Chats are encrypted in transit but stored on our servers so you can read them across devices. We do not routinely read private messages. We may review messages and photos when they are reported, when needed to investigate abuse, or when the law requires.

6. How long we keep data

We keep your information while your account is active. If you ask us to delete your account, we remove your profile, photos, matches and messages from the live Service within 30 days. Copies in backups are overwritten on the normal backup cycle, and we may keep limited records where the law requires or to handle abuse (for example, a record that an account was banned).

7. Your rights

Under the Kenya Data Protection Act, 2019, and similar laws, you may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal data, object to or restrict certain processing, receive a copy of your data, and withdraw consent at any time. You can edit most profile details, update or stop sharing location, and delete individual photos inside the app. For anything else, or to delete your account, email support@mapenzitele.co.ke from the address on your account. We respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response you can complain to the Office of the Data Protection Commissioner.

8. Security

Passwords are hashed with bcrypt, traffic uses HTTPS, access to our systems is restricted, and we rate-limit sign-in attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password and tell us straight away if you suspect your account has been accessed by someone else.

9. International processing

Our hosting providers may process data in data centres outside Kenya. Where that happens we rely on providers that apply appropriate safeguards for personal data.

10. Age requirement

mapenziTELE is strictly for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor is using the Service, report it to support@mapenzitele.co.ke and we will remove the account.

11. Cookies and local storage

We only use essential browser storage (your login token). We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. If the changes are significant we will notify you in the app or by email before they take effect. The effective date above shows the latest version.
