Safety Policy

Effective 20 October 2026 · Operated by Jatelo Technologies Limited
Your safety comes first. This policy explains how we protect you, what we do not allow, and how to stay safe when you meet people.

Our commitments

  • Adults only. Everyone on mapenziTELE must be 18 or over. We remove accounts we believe belong to minors and report child exploitation to the authorities.
  • Your location stays private. Other members see only an approximate distance rounded to the nearest kilometre, never your coordinates or address. You can stop sharing location any time by turning off location in your device settings.
  • You control who you talk to. You only chat with people you have matched with, and you can unmatch at any time to end a conversation.
  • We act on reports. We review reports and take action, from warnings to permanent bans.

What is not allowed

We have zero tolerance for:

  • anyone under 18, or any sexual content involving minors;
  • harassment, threats, stalking, hate or discrimination;
  • sexual violence, coercion, or sharing intimate images without consent;
  • scams, romance fraud, requests for money, or promotion of products and services;
  • fake profiles and impersonation;
  • solicitation of paid sexual services, trafficking or exploitation;
  • nudity or sexually explicit photos.

The full rules are in our Terms of Service.

Before you meet someone

  • Take your time. Chat in the app first. Do a video call before meeting so you know they are who their photos show.
  • Check them out. Look for consistency between their photos, story and what they say. A reverse image search on their photos can reveal stolen pictures.
  • Keep personal details private until you trust the person: your home or work address, full name, financial information, and daily routine.
  • Never send money or share bank, mobile-money or card details, however good the story is.

When you meet

  • Meet in a public, busy place the first few times. Do not invite someone to your home or go to theirs.
  • Tell a friend or family member who you are meeting, where, and when you expect to be back. Share your live location with them.
  • Arrange your own transport so you can leave when you want.
  • Watch your drinks and stay in control. Do not accept drinks you did not see poured, and limit alcohol.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave. You do not owe anyone an explanation.
  • Consent is required for everything, every time, and can be withdrawn at any moment. Practise safe sex.

Warning signs of a scam or a dangerous person

  • Asks for money, gifts, airtime or “emergency” help, or pushes you toward an investment.
  • Refuses to video call or always has an excuse not to meet in public.
  • Moves quickly to love, then to another app, then to money.
  • Pressures you for intimate photos or threatens to share them.
  • Gets angry when you set boundaries, or tries to control who you see.
  • Their story, photos or details do not add up.

If something goes wrong

If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number first (999 or 112 in Kenya). Gender-based violence helpline in Kenya: 1195.
  • Unmatch from the Matches tab to end the conversation straight away.
  • Report a person or message to support@mapenzitele.co.ke. Include their name and what happened, and screenshots if you have them. Mark urgent cases as “URGENT” in the subject line.
  • If you have been a victim of a crime, report it to the police. We will cooperate with lawful requests from the authorities.
  • If someone is threatening to share your private images, do not pay. Stop contact, keep the evidence, and report them to us and to the police.

What happens after you report

We review the report, and may remove content, warn the person, restrict or ban their account. We will not share your identity with the person you reported unless the law requires it. We cannot always tell you the outcome for privacy reasons, but every report is looked at.
